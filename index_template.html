— about

I like understanding LLMs inside the black box: how they work, how to make them faster, and how to tell whether their outputs hold up. Most of my time goes to reinforcement learning, mechanistic interpretability, inference engineering, and agentic frameworks.

At HiLabs I'm setting up our first LLM-inference GPU infrastructure: scalable serving for fine-tuned small language models on downstream tasks, with KV-cache-aware routing to save tokens and speed up inference. Alongside that I work on agentic memory, and I contribute to GNU Octave through GSoC 2026.

Happy to talk about inference systems, interpretability, or evaluation. Reach out via email or X.
