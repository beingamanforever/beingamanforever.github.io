I am a Data Scientist at HiLabs and an independent researcher associated with the Vision and Learning Group at IIT Roorkee. I build and study AI agents, reinforcement learning methods, agentic memory, and document intelligence. My recent work includes RDAN-GRPO for instruction following and notSoSmartOCR for extracting structured content from complex documents.

My papers on multilingual hallucination detection, MycoGate-VL, and position bias in language models, Mixing Matters, have been accepted at SHROOM-Visions at EMNLP 2026 and New in ML at NeurIPS 2026, respectively.

I graduated from IIT Roorkee in 2026, previously worked on generative engine optimisation at Info Edge, and contributed to GNU Octave through Google Summer of Code. Outside work, I run, swim, and powerlift.
