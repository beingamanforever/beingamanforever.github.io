I work on AI agents, reinforcement learning, AI safety and document intelligence, combining research with building practical systems. Recently I tested whether a prompt-injection defense trained in English survives other languages (Lost in Injection), studied what offline audits can and cannot verify about agents (Post-Audit Mirage), and built notSoSmartOCR, document extraction you can inspect, and LevelHead, training-free page orientation for any model. I work at HiLabs and am associated with the Vision and Learning Group at IIT Roorkee.

I graduated from IIT Roorkee in 2026, previously worked on generative engine optimisation at Info Edge, and contributed to GNU Octave through Google Summer of Code. Outside work, I run, swim, and powerlift.

My papers Mixing Matters, on position bias across Transformer, Mamba, and hybrid language models, and Post-Audit Mirage, on the identifiability limits of offline agent verification, were accepted at the PriGM and Who Verifies the Agents? workshops at NeurIPS 2026. MycoGate-VL, on multilingual hallucination detection, was accepted at SHROOM-Visions at EMNLP 2026.

Now. Making notSoSmartOCR better at the pages it doubts, taking LevelHead further, and learning GPU programming (CS193G and PPMP, with a focus on CUDA). Happy to talk about agents, safety evaluations and document AI.
