CV

I work on AI agents, reinforcement learning, agentic memory, and document intelligence, combining research with building practical systems. My recent work includes notSoSmartOCR for extracting structured content from complex documents, Lost in Injection for testing whether prompt-injection defenses survive translation, and RDAN-GRPO for instruction following. I work at HiLabs as an AI Engineer and am associated with the Vision and Learning Group at IIT Roorkee.

I graduated from IIT Roorkee in 2026, previously worked on generative engine optimisation at Info Edge, and contributed to GNU Octave through Google Summer of Code. Outside work, I run, swim, and powerlift.
