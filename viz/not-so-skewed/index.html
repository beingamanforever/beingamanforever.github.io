How the angle is found

The four steps of the method, computed live on the input above. Text lines become a bright line through the centre of the Fourier spectrum; the angle of that line is the skew.

1

Binarize and pad

B
B=T15×15(255−I),B∈{0,1}N×N

Resize to height H, convert to gray, pad with white to an N×N square that the FFT handles fast, then a Gaussian adaptive threshold (C = −10) keeps ink as 1.

2

2D Fourier magnitude

M
M=|shift(F2{B})|

Log-scaled for display. The dashed circle has radius W, the shaded fan is the search range, and the orange ray is the angle being tested.

Robustness sweep

Treats the current image as straight, tilts it to 25 known angles across the search range, and scores both estimators with the paper's metrics: average error (AED), share within 0.1° (CE), and worst error (WE).
