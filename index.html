CV

I work on AI agents, reinforcement learning, agentic memory, and document intelligence, combining research with building practical systems. My recent work includes notSoSmartOCR for extracting structured content from complex documents, Lost in Injection for testing whether prompt-injection defenses survive translation, and RDAN-GRPO for instruction following. I work at HiLabs as an AI Engineer and am associated with the Vision and Learning Group at IIT Roorkee.

I graduated from IIT Roorkee in 2026, previously worked on generative engine optimisation at Info Edge, and contributed to GNU Octave through Google Summer of Code. Outside work, I run, swim, and powerlift.

Projects

notSoSmartOCR

Built a document-intelligence product that turns complex scans into structured, reviewable outputs. It combines orientation correction, layout detection, Falcon OCR 1.5, and selective LLM refinement for handwriting and ambiguous regions, and shows page-level evidence, per-block confidence, tables, and Markdown output.

Drishti

A scalable remote-sensing pipeline integrating EarthMind-4B, RemoteSAM, and a BERT classifier for unified satellite-image analysis. DRISHTI stands for Deep Remote-sensing Intelligence System for Holistic Task Integration. Awarded a silver medal at Inter IIT Tech Meet 14.0.

Tech-Enhanced AI Interview Learning Platform

Developed an interview practice platform that generates diverse, topic-specific questions and uses NLP to analyse spoken responses, identify grammatical errors, and provide corrections after the interview.
