RDAN-GRPO

Reinforcement learning

Rubric Decoupled Advantage Normalisation GRPO is a rubric-based reinforcement learning repository for aligning LLMs with complex, open-domain instruction-following tasks. It improves performance on public instruction-following benchmarks.

Mixing Matters

Accepted · New in ML at NeurIPS 2026

Mixing Matters studies how moving the same evidence through a long context changes retrieval across Transformer, Mamba, and hybrid models. The measured position-bias curves vary with the model, prompt, and task; the study does not isolate architecture as the cause.

MycoGate-VL

Accepted · SHROOM-Visions at EMNLP 2026

Detects and categorizes hallucinated character spans in vision-language responses across English, French, Italian, and Chinese. Combines multilingual detectors, a learned clean-document gate, visual category supervision, and scorer-aware output serialization.
